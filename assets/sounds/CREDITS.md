# Commute sound library

These ten excerpts are adapted from seven recordings released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Credits are preserved here and in the app. Original creators do not endorse Commute.

- **Calm Piano 1 (Vaporware)** — The Cynic Project / cynicmusic. [Source and license](https://opengameart.org/content/calm-piano-1-vaporware).
- **Exploration Theme** — Cleyton Kauffman. [Source and license](https://opengameart.org/content/exploration-theme).
- **Happy moments** — Centurion_of_war. [Source and license](https://opengameart.org/content/happy-moments).
- **Forest Ambience 1** — deadrobotmusic. [Source and license](https://freesound.org/people/deadrobotmusic/sounds/687053/).
- **gentle rainfall.wav** — rasunter255. [Source and license](https://freesound.org/people/rasunter255/sounds/416227/).
- **Calm ocean waves** — SamsterBirdies. [Source and license](https://freesound.org/people/SamsterBirdies/sounds/578524/).
- **Fireplace Fire Crackling** — RyanKingArt. [Source and license](https://freesound.org/people/RyanKingArt/sounds/717579/).

SOURCES.json records the exact downloads, excerpt positions, processing and SHA-256 hashes. Freesound sources use the publicly available high-quality MP3 previews. Rebuild with `npm run sounds:prepare`; normal playback and mixing are entirely local.
